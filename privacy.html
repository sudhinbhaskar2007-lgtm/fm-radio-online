Privacy Policy

Who we are

This policy covers mizhinews.co.uk, which runs Mizhi UK Malayalam News and the Mizhi FM online radio. You can contact us about privacy at mizhinews@gmail.com.

What we collect

You don't need an account to read the news or listen to the radio. We don't ask for your name or email, and we don't sell personal data.

  • Choices saved on your device. Your privacy choice and radio volume are kept in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to us.
  • Server logs. Our hosting (Cloudflare) and our radio stream server receive your IP address and browser details when you visit or listen, like any website. These are used for security and to keep the service running.
  • Messages you send us. If you email or message us news, photos or requests, we use them to reply and, where you've asked, to publish them.

Services from other companies

  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on the page.
  • Open-Meteo supplies the London weather, and ExchangeRate-API supplies the £ to ₹ rate. Your browser contacts them directly, so they see your IP address.
  • Google AdSense shows advertisements in clearly labelled spaces. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud.

Advertising and cookies

Visitors in the UK, the EEA and Switzerland are asked by Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. If you don't agree, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use cookies only for things like frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings at the bottom of the home page. To read how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can manage personalised Google ads at Google Ad Settings.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email mizhinews@gmail.com. If you're not happy with our reply, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

This site is for a general audience and is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
