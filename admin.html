Add a news story വാർത്ത ചേർക്കാൻ

  1. Click News / വാർത്ത, then Add an entry (you can drag it to the top).
  2. Fill in the headline, date and category. Malayalam boxes are optional.
  3. Upload a photo if you have one.
  4. Tick Top story to show it big, or Breaking for the red ticker.
  5. Click Save. The website updates by itself in about 1 to 2 minutes.

Add a photo album ചിത്രങ്ങൾ ചേർക്കാൻ

  1. Click Photo gallery / ചിത്രങ്ങൾ, then Add an entry.
  2. Type the album name and date, e.g. "Onam 2026, Manchester".
  3. Upload the photos (you can pick several at once). The first photo is the cover.
  4. Click Save.

Good to know

  1. Only people the owner has invited can sign in.
  2. Use your own photos, or photos you have permission to use. Please don't copy articles or pictures from other news websites.
  3. Smaller photos load faster: about 1600 pixels wide is plenty.
  4. Made a mistake? Every save is kept, so the owner can always undo it.
